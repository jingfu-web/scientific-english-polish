## Reading limitations and selection

All files were text-extracted by page/slide and their subject coverage inspected. The long Zhiwu lecture has a damaged Chinese text layer; writing-component pages 12–54 were additionally rendered and visually checked. Not every page of mathematical history or every figure was visually transcribed. Extracted formulas are not a reliable transcription of the originals. The skill uses stable editorial principles and does not certify the mathematical examples or bibliographic examples in these lectures.

The introductory research-philosophy, productivity, professional-service, mathematical-history, and journal-process narratives are not runtime polishing instructions. Detailed proof construction and comprehensive peer review are outside this skill's normal scope.

## Corrections and non-adoption

- The bilingual notes' item 2 contains `has been received much attention`; this is not a valid model for an active `has received` construction.
- Item 49 gives `enforces` in the English explanation but `enforce` in the Chinese explanation for a singular procedure; examples must be checked, not copied by label.
- The long lecture's p. 252 retains `Uzam and Zhou provides` even after its arrow. An after-arrow example is not necessarily fully corrected.
- Some rewriting demonstrations introduce future predictions or stronger implications. This skill instead preserves the supplied claim and queries substantive gaps.
- Different sources prefer different voices, abbreviation quotas, and reference styles. See [style-decisions.md](style-decisions.md) for resolutions.

The workflow, edit levels, output contract, LaTeX preservation requirements, semantic comparison pass, and new examples are implementation choices added for reliable reuse, rather than quotations from the lectures.
